The build-up to All In 2026 was confusing for most fans. Plans seemed to consistently change with involvement from the Death Riders, a group Will Ospreay was apart of, and heavy speculation over who would be a good guy and who would be a bad guy during the match as most fans outside the UK saw Ospreay as the bad guy and Omega as the good guy but fans within the UK (Where the event was happening) viewed it the other way around. 

Regardless of who thought what, everyone knew this match was going to be one for the ages with many believing this is finally where Will Ospreay would kick out of the One Winged Angel, ending the 14 year long streak it had of winning every single match. 

Our match began with two beautifully authentic entrances, Will Ospreays featuring a voice-over of how proud his mother was of him with a cinematic walk-out of him passing by all of those who were prominent in his career. Meanwhile Omega had his entrance voiced over by the actor of All Might from My Hero Academia, signifying the passing of the torch to come.

The match was a brutally bloody affair, with Omega dominating the first half of the match brutalising Ospreay at every single turn. Each flurry of offence Ospreay brought up was drowned out by Omega hitting back twice as hard, the playing field was eventually evened after Ospreay hit Omega with his own finisher, the hidden blade.

Yet this still did not stop Omega, who after a devastating series of moves hit Ospreay with the One Winged Angel. The crowd, who was so accustomed to this ending the match dropped to their chairs in defeat, a tense atmosphere in the stadium was present as they hoped Ospreay would defy what many before him could not do. The referee began his count and at the very last millisecond Ospreay kicked out causing the stadium and those at home to erupt in joy. Ospreay would then go on to win this match, becoming the AEW World Champion which he still is to this day.

This moment though could not have been as thrilling as it was if the OWA wasn't built up as much as it had been, investing the crowd into the power of this move which paid off in a huge fashion for them. It signifies how professional wrestling is a never-ending story where each bit of detail and every single day counts towards the over-arching narrative.
