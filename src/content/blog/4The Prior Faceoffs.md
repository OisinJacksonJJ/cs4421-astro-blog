The duo was set to meet at Wrestle Kingdom 17 in 2024, Japans largest wrestling show of the year. The match that unfolded is regarded as one of the best in wrestling history, lasting well over 30 minutes and had the crowd mesmerised by the fight unfolding before them. Ultimately, Will Ospreay was defeated by the One Winged Angel, causing many to continue to deem Ospreay as the "Lesser Than".

Later on that year the two were scheduled for a rematch at the cross promotional (AEW x NJPW) Pay Per View: Forbidden Door (My first Pay Per View I bought!).Yet again the duo had an unbeliveable showcase which many deem as good as their Wrestle Kingdom match, this time Will Ospreay picked up the win and the two went their seperate ways.

Following this massive win, Ospreay continued his work in New Japan where he became the face of their promotion, losing the stigma he had of filling Omegas shoes. In 2025 he was signed to AEW and quickly became a fan favourite. Instantly fans began to speculate as to when the next face-off would occur, but injuries derailed these plans until 2026 when Ospreay won the Owen Hart Cup Tournament which guaranteed him a title shot against the World Champion at All In 2026. This champion? Kenny Omega.
