# ADR-001: Selecting Astro for the Blog
## Status: Accepted (Date: 2026-9-9)
## Context
In the initial planning stages of the project, a platform was required in order to host the blog.
The platform also needed to be beginner friendly to account for our inexperience
## Decision
We chose Astro as the platform for our blog due to it's fast output, lightweight static pages and content driven values
## Consequences
- Positive: Blog was scaffolded relatively easily, content is outputted fast.
- Negative: Future user interactivity or heavy duty applications will be difficult to implement.
